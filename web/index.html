ONE JUMP. MANY PROBLEMS.

  • JUMP: tap to flop toward the loose ball. Your lean changes the jump.
  • SHOOT: grab the ball, hold JUMP, then release. The yellow meter is your possession timer.
  • DUNK: with the ball, launch toward the rim and slam. It has a cooldown and can miss. Without the ball, it is a blocking lunge.
  • LOCAL: gold uses W / S; red uses ↑ / ↓. On phones, use the buttons on each side.
  • WIN: first to 5. Rainbow balls count for 2. Courts, bodies, and balls change each round.
  • ONLINE: both players share a seeded match through your configured server. A disconnect ends the match.
