HOW TO PLAY

  • JUMP: tap once to leap. Holding does not repeat jumps. Your sway changes direction.
  • SHOOT: grab the ball, hold JUMP to charge, release to throw. A longer hold adds a little power; the bar above your head shows charge.
  • DUNK: with the ball, leap toward the rim. It has a cooldown and can miss. Without the ball, it is a blocking lunge.
  • TEAMS: normal modes are 2v2: one button controls both teammates. Use 1V1 for one player per side.
  • LOCAL: gold W / S, red ↑ / ↓. Phones use the buttons on each side.
  • WIN: first to 5. Colorful balls count for 2. Courts, bodies, jerseys, skins, balls, and hoops change each round.
  • ONLINE: both players share a seeded match through your server. A disconnect ends the match.
